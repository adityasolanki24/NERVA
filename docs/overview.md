# Overview
NERVA is an inteligent bipedal robotic platform designed to exhibit behaviour patterns analogous to emotional expressins observed in biological organisms while also maintaining dynamic locomotive abilities and balance.
This platform cant simulate livign emotions psychologically. Instead NERVA models emotions as a structured internal latent state that modulates behaviour selection , policy arbitration and expressive posture in a physically grounded manner.
The objective is to investigate how internal regulatory states can influence embodied robotic behaviour without compromising control stability.
